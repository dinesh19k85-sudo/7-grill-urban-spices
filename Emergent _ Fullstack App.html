Chapter · Ignition

Fire.

Every plate begins with heat. Charcoal-fed tandoors, cast-iron griddles, oil that sings the moment a lollipop hits it. Nothing on this menu whispers.

02
Chapter · Blend

Spice.

Ground the same evening, warmed slow, layered heavy. Punjab knows its masalas — turmeric, kashmiri chilli, roasted cumin, kasuri methi — and we don't hold back.

Table.

Kitty afternoons that stretch into evenings. Birthdays with 40 relatives and one candle. First dates. Last exams. We set the table long — you decide how loud.
